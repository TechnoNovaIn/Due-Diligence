Limitations of the encumbrance evidence
  • An Encumbrance Certificate reflects only what was registered against the identifiers used to obtain it, for the period it certifies.
  • An equitable mortgage created by deposit of title deeds is frequently not registered and may therefore not appear.
  • Unregistered agreements, family arrangements, tenancies and oral claims do not appear.
  • No certificate has been verified against the Sub-Registrar's own record.
